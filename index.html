React to your own videos

Switch to Video mode and pick a clip from Photos or Files. It plays while you record, beside you or behind you.

The clip's sound is mixed into the finished take, so with headphones on your microphone only catches you. Video mode needs only the front camera, so it runs on every supported iPhone. Affectcam never downloads from websites; only react to videos you have the right to use.

Questions

Which iPhones can record both cameras at once?

Recording both cameras needs an iPhone that supports it; most iPhones from the XS and XR on do. On other devices the Cameras option is off and Video mode still works.

Does it work on iPad?

The app is made for iPhone. It runs on iPad too, and Video mode works there with the front camera.

Can I paste a YouTube or TikTok link?

No. Affectcam only plays videos that are already on your device or in Files. Saving a video yourself and picking it works.

Is the clip's sound in my reaction video?

Yes. The microphone records you, and the clip's sound is added at the right moment when you play or export the take.

What does the expression track measure?

Smile, surprise, brow raise, jaw open and blinks, computed from face landmarks on the device about 15 times a second. It is a playful measure of expressions, not a reading of feelings.
