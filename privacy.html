Privacy Policy

Affectcam · 30 September 2026

Affectcam collects no information about you. It has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing to me or to anyone else.

Camera and microphone

The app uses the camera to record video and the microphone to record your voice. While you record, it computes person masks and facial expression values from the front camera on the device, using Apple's on-device frameworks. None of this leaves your phone. The microphone is optional; without it, takes are recorded silently.

Your takes

Videos, their expression tracks and any video you import to react to are stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting a take, or removing the app, erases them.

Exports

When you export a take, you choose where it goes: your photo library, or any destination in the share sheet. The app asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you save there. What happens to a file after you share it is up to the app or service you send it to.

Videos you import

The app only plays videos that are already on your device or in Files. It never downloads from websites or other services. Only react to videos you have the right to use.

The optional purchase

Affectcam Pro is bought through the App Store, and Apple processes the payment. The app only learns from Apple whether you own it and keeps that one fact on the device.

Internet

The app has no network code of its own. The only connections it makes go through Apple's StoreKit, to buy or restore the purchase and to confirm you own it.

Children

The app records nothing about anyone, so it records nothing about children either.
